Core principles
OWL
Observe, Wait, Listen
Let her lead every interaction
Watch what she's interested in before you say anything
Count 5 seconds silently after any sound or gesture she makes
Her focus is the topic — follow it, don't redirect
Even a look toward something counts as communication — name it
4S
The four S's
How to shape your language around her
Say less — match her level, then go one word above it
Stress key words — "Mama is HERE" — pause on what matters
Go slow — leave gaps. She needs processing time
Show — pair every word with a sign, gesture or object
→+
Expand, don't correct
Build on whatever she offers
She says "Mama" → you say "Mama is here!" or "Mama, juice"
She signs "eat" → you say "Eat! You want to eat, yes!"
She points → you name it, sign it, celebrate it
Never "say it properly" — honour every attempt, always
Q?
Reduce questions
Comments invite, questions pressure
Instead of "What's that?" → "Oh, a ball! Big red ball."
Instead of "What do you want?" → "Juice or milk?" (offer choices)
Aim for max 1 question per 5 minutes of play
♻
Routines are therapy
Use daily moments intentionally
Bath time: name body parts, water, soap — same words every time
Meals: name food before giving it, use "more?" with the sign
Potty: consistent phrase every time — "Toilet time → sit down → well done"
Bedtime: predictable book + song builds language through repetition

Makaton — core signs to reinforce now

🤟Priority signs

  • More — fists together, tap twice. Use at every meal and play
  • Finished / all done — hands flat, flick outward. End every activity with it
  • Help — one open hand on top of the other, lift both up
  • Toilet — index and middle fingers together, tap wrist twice
  • Eat / food — fingers bunched together, tap lips
  • Drink — thumb to mouth, tilt fist as if drinking
  • Sleep — hands together, tilt head and rest on them
  • No — index and middle finger tap on thumb, like a beak closing

🚽Potty training — consistent language

Use the exact same phrase and sign every single time.

  • "Toilet time" — say it + sign toilet
  • "Sit down" — say it + gesture to seat
  • "Well done! Finished!" — say + sign finished, big celebration
  • "Wash hands" — say + mime rubbing hands together

Daily environment checklist

✅Set up for success every day

  • Screen time only as post-therapy wind-down — not during learning windows
  • TV off during meals and play — reduce auditory competition
  • Get face-to-face at her eye level when speaking
  • Narrate your own actions: "Mama is washing the cup"
  • Celebrate every attempt — a sound, a look, a gesture all count
  • One person speaks at a time — no overlapping voices
  • Consistent bedtime routine: same books, same songs, every night
